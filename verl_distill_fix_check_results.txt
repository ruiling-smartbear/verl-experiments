== Appendix B: why DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B is loaded as Qwen2TokenizerFast here
Its tokenizer_config.json on the Hub declares "tokenizer_class": "LlamaTokenizerFast"
(checked 2026-08-31; the older R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B declares the same, Qwen/Qwen3-8B
declares Qwen2Tokenizer). Under transformers 5 that load path decodes this checkpoint's
ids without spaces and strips the ｜/▁ marker characters, which breaks marker-exact tool
parsing downstream; loading the same files as Qwen2TokenizerFast decodes correctly and
yields identical ids. None of the checkpoint's 27 Hub discussions mention it. Outside
verl's scope — a checkpoint-side issue.
